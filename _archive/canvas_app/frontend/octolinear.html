Octolinear Layout

The lattice is rendered with octolinear constraints: edges are either axis-aligned (90°) or diagonal (45°), determined by the antipode grading of the KKT multiplier components.

  • x-coordinate: size + assocDefect(cd) (even components → 90°)
  • y-coordinate: leftWeight - rightWeight (odd components → 45°)
  • 90° edges: Pure even or pure odd component changes
  • 45° edges: Mixed even+odd component changes
